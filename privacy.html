Privacy Policy

Effective: 13 September 2026

This policy explains how Tivals AI, powered by tivalsdeveloper, handles information when you use the application.

Information we process

  • Account details such as your name and email address when you register.
  • Messages and prompts you submit to the AI service.
  • Conversation history saved locally on your device.
  • Gmail message metadata and snippets only when you deliberately connect Gmail and request Gmail features.

How information is used

We use information to authenticate your account, provide AI responses, maintain your conversations, operate Gmail features you enable, prevent misuse, and improve reliability.

Google user data

Gmail access is optional and uses read-only permission. When enabled, Tivals AI retrieves recent message details so it can show alerts or answer your email-related request. Relevant email details may be sent to the configured AI service to generate the answer you requested. Tivals AI does not send, modify, or delete Gmail messages. Monitoring runs only while the application is open.

Storage and service providers

Authentication is provided through Supabase. AI requests are processed through the configured Tivals AI backend and its model provider. Some settings and chat history are stored in your browser. These providers may process limited information required to deliver their services.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared only with service providers needed to operate requested features, when legally required, or to protect the service and its users.

Your choices

You can avoid connecting Gmail, stop email monitoring, disconnect Gmail, clear browser-stored conversations, or sign out. You may revoke Google access from your Google Account permissions.

Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee complete security. Information is retained only as needed to operate the service, comply with legal obligations, and resolve security issues.

Children

Tivals AI is not intended for children under 13 without appropriate parent, guardian, or school supervision.

Changes

We may update this policy when the application or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact tivalsdeveloper@gmail.com.
