What can it do?

AI conversations

Ask a question or describe a task in chat. Image and voice features depend on the available model and your plan.

YouTube results

Search for videos in Telegram and open playable previews when Telegram and the video allow playback. Example: /youtube Python basics.

Email assistance

With your permission, search and read Gmail, prepare a relevant reply and ask for your confirmation before sending it.

Reminders

Set a personal reminder and view upcoming reminders with Telegram commands.

Business knowledge

Add your service details, opening hours, specialists and frequent answers so replies reflect your business.

Website widget

Make your business assistant available on an authorized website through a domain-restricted embed.

Get started

Open @Tivalsdeveloperbot and launch its dashboard.
Create a bot through Telegram’s verified @BotFather, or connect a bot you already own.
Open the mini app, set the bot’s name and purpose, then connect only the accounts and website you need.
Send a test message to your bot and review its reply in Telegram.

A connected bot account is one linked to Tivals AI. Bot creation itself happens through Telegram; connection totals do not represent all BotFather creations.
